When a field validator targeted a section directly (e.g. `Annotated[TgConfig, V.check(...)]`), that section was constructed for real from each source's own partial data during the field pass. A nested field with an un-callable `default_factory` (e.g. `proxy: ProxyConfig = field(default_factory=ProxyConfig)` where `ProxyConfig` requires arguments) then leaked a bare `TypeError` (e.g. `ProxyConfig.__init__() missing 2 required positional arguments: 'url' and 'port'`) under the wrong field path instead of a normal, readable "Missing required field" error. Such fields are now treated as required during the field pass too, so their absence is reported like any other missing field.
